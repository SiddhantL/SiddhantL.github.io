Minro is a proactive customer success platform that helps companies understand their users and take action when those users need help. We connect product behavior with customer context so teams can spot friction, identify churn risk, and reach out with something relevant.

The product brings together customer profiles, retention analytics, and configurable agents. Teams can use behavioral signals to trigger personalized outreach, review messages before sending or automate delivery, and track replies and return behavior. Experiments help compare approaches and inform what to try next.

We also work directly with our clients' teams to understand their products and workflows. That means defining meaningful activation and retention metrics, connecting the right data, and shaping customer success workflows around the people using their product.

My work as CTO spans data integrations, event-driven agents, personalized communication, and outcome measurement. The platform is built with FastAPI, PostgreSQL, background workers, and React.

Backed by Y Combinator (F25).
